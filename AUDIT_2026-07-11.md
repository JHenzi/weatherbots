# System Audit — 2026-07-11

Audit of decisions/trades history, daily MAE metrics, weights, and confidence pipeline.

## TL;DR

The forecasts are fine and improving. The trading is dead because **confidence is
computed from provider disagreement (sigma), not from realized accuracy** — so it can
never improve, and it's gated at 0.60 while it averages ~0.38. Google-weather and
weather.gov are not the culprits (learned weights ~0.001–0.007, effectively zero); the
real damage comes from **unreliable providers still inflating sigma**, which is what
feeds confidence.

## Why confidence "never improves" — it structurally can't

In `intraday_pulse.py` (~line 1540), `conf_final = spread_conf × (0.5 + 0.5 × skill_conf)`:

- `spread_conf` comes from `_confidence_from_spread` (~line 200): a hard ramp — 1.0 at
  sigma ≤ 1.5°F, **zero at sigma ≥ 3.0°F**, capped at 0.9.
- Average sigma is ~2.15°F → spread_conf ≈ 0.57 → conf_final ≈ 0.40 after the skill
  and condition multipliers.
- Realized MAE (the thing that's improving) enters only weakly via `skill_conf`.

So even as accuracy improves, confidence stays flat at 0.38–0.40 — permanently below
the `--min-confidence 0.60` gate in `scripts/run_trade.sh:52` (blended with conviction
0.7/0.3 in `kalshi_trader.py` ~line 1544).

## The "bad sources break it" intuition — half-right

Google-weather and weather.gov have near-zero learned weights, so they barely touch the
consensus prediction. **But sigma is a different pipeline**: the max-source-divergence
guardrail (`intraday_pulse.py` ~1520–1526) widens sigma based on *any* non-rejected
source, regardless of weight. A provider the weights already learned to ignore (e.g.
weatherapi in NY at 6.67°F MAE) can still blow out sigma → zero confidence → skip.
Unreliable providers do break the system — via the confidence path, not the weight path.

## Recommendations (in order of impact)

1. **Tie confidence to realized skill.** Consensus MAE of 1.45°F over a ~2°F bucket is
   a real edge; confidence should rise as trailing MAE falls instead of being hostage
   to provider disagreement.
2. **Make sigma weight-/MAE-aware.** Exclude or downweight sources with poor trailing
   city-specific MAE from spread and the divergence guardrail — the weights already
   know who to trust; sigma doesn't.
3. **Recheck the 0.60 gate.** With confidence capped near ~0.9 and typically ~0.4,
   0.60 means "almost never trade." Lower it, or fix the confidence scale first.
4. **"Riskier trades under the right conditions" is half-built already** — the
   condition-aware multiplier and the LinUCB bandit exist, but they multiply a number
   stuck below the gate, so they never get to act. Fixing 1–3 turns them back on.
